3. Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

A. What we do not collect

This website does not create a user account, does not require a login, and does not maintain a user database for citizen profiles.

यह वेबसाइट उपयोगकर्ता खाता नहीं बनाती, लॉगिन नहीं मांगती, और नागरिक प्रोफाइल डेटाबेस नहीं रखती।

B. Data entered by users

Some forms on the site, such as the grievance draft tool, may ask for names, phone numbers, EPIC/reference information, and complaint text. This information is processed in the browser for local drafting only and is not submitted to a remote backend or stored by the site operator as a complaint database.
